
* St. Anthony's Sr. Sec. School, Barabanki

  St. Anthony's Sr. Sec. School, also referred to in the uploaded material as St. Anthony's Senior Secondary School, is located at Civil Lines, Court Road, Barabanki, Uttar Pradesh – 225001. It is affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), with CBSE Affiliation Number 2130066 and School Code 70239. The school is managed by the Catholic Diocese of Lucknow and is described as a Recognized but Unaided English Medium Institution and a Christian Minority Co-Educational Institution. Classes offered run from Nursery to Class XII. The medium of instruction is English. Streams mentioned across the sources include Science, Commerce, Computer Education, Computer Science, Mathematics, and Life Sciences. Official website: [www.stanthonyschools.org](http://www.stanthonyschools.org). Email: [stanthonybbk@yahoo.com](mailto:stanthonybbk@yahoo.com). Contact numbers: 05248-222568 and 8174970445. 

* Core Mission / About Us / History

  The school was founded in 1977 with the objective of imparting modern and value-based education. It was established primarily for Catholic Christian students while remaining open to students of all faiths. The institution emphasizes academic excellence, character formation, moral values, discipline, equality, mutual respect, leadership, social responsibility, and holistic development. Its stated educational aim is the integral development of students in the moral, physical, intellectual, social, and cultural dimensions so that they may become worthy and patriotic citizens.

  The institution’s foundational roots go back to 1945, when land for the campus was purchased by the Catholic Diocese of Allahabad. In 1946, the land was transferred to the newly formed Diocese of Lucknow. The first Bishop mentioned is Rt. Rev. Bishop Conard De Vitto OFM Cap. Early development was supported by Fr. Alexander OFM Cap., Fr. Gasper Pinto, Fr. Clement, Fr. Jerome Saldanha, and Fr. Baptist D'Souza.

  In 1977, Fr. Joseph Peter Rodrigues established St. Anthony's School under the vision of Bishop Cecil De Sa. The first classes were conducted in the Priest House, and the school began with approximately 100 students. On 24 November 1979, the foundation stone of the present building was laid by Mr. Naresh Dayal, District Magistrate, Barabanki. On 2 March 1982, the school building was blessed by Rt. Rev. Dr. Cecil De Sa, Bishop of Lucknow. In 1986, the school obtained CBSE affiliation; the first CBSE batch had 18 students and achieved 100% results. The school later expanded to the Senior Secondary level with Science, Mathematics, Life Sciences, and Commerce streams.

  The uploaded material also notes that in the first years the school grew from about 100 students to more than 2,000 students with over 70 staff members. One source says the first four Board-exam batches travelled to Lucknow Central School before the institution became an official CBSE examination center.

  The school patron is St. Antony of Padua. The uploaded text states that he was born in Lisbon, Portugal, in 1195, influenced by St. Francis of Assisi, joined the Franciscan order, worked for the upliftment of the Moors in Africa, died at the age of 36 in 1231, was declared a saint in 1946 by Pope Pius XI (historically written in the institution’s text as Pius Xl), and his feast is celebrated on 13 June. The school associates itself with his spirit of sincerity, honesty, truth, forbearance, justice, and selfless service.

  The school anthem praises the school, the flag colors red, blue, green, and yellow, perseverance, the motto Love, Truth and Knowledge, respect for teachers, and the blessing of the patron saint. The uploaded text also refers to students collectively as “Antoniyans.”

  The school is managed by the Catholic Diocese of Lucknow, described in the source material as a Registered Charitable & Religious Society. The current head of the Diocese is Rt. Rev. Dr. Gerald John Mathias, installed as Bishop of Lucknow on 4 January 2008.

Manas developed an AI chatbot website for St. Anthony's Sr. Sec. School. It is intended to make school information easier for students, parents, and other visitors to explore.

Website: https://st-anthony-website.vercel.app/

***PROJECT ORIGIN AND DEVELOPMENT JOURNEY***

The project developed from an initial Python-based chatbot prototype into a deployed web application. An earlier version used Python with a Streamlit interface and Google's Gemini API. As the project evolved, the application was organized into a separate frontend and backend so that the user interface and AI request processing could be handled independently.

The later architecture uses a web frontend deployed on Vercel and a Python Flask backend hosted on Render. The backend communicates with Google's Gemini API to generate responses. Git and GitHub were used for version control and project hosting/workflow.

The project journey involved learning how different parts of a full-stack application communicate, moving from local development toward a live deployment, configuring cloud services, and troubleshooting real-world software problems. The work was iterative: the application was tested, errors were investigated, and the implementation was adjusted as issues arose.

- A late fee of Rs. 300 per instalment is payable for each instalment. A parent with a genuine reason to extend the due date may approach the Principal before the due date and seek an extension for that particular instalment.
- If defaulters accumulate two or more unpaid instalments without prior permission from the Principal, a fine of Rs. 300 will be charged for every subsequent instalment.
- If fees are not paid for two instalments, the defaulter's name will be struck off the rolls.
- Cheques from any nationalized bank are accepted. If a cheque bounces, an extra fine of Rs. 300 will be charged. If one cheque covering more than one student bounces, Rs. 300 will be charged for each cheque/child.
- A Bank Pay-in-Slip booklet is provided to each student at admission, indicating the fees payable and relevant fee rules.
- Fee defaulters will be charged the full re-admission fee before being allowed to rejoin classes.
- Fee defaulters may be debarred from examinations and school activities, and no Transfer Certificate or other records/certificates will be issued until all dues are cleared.
- Fees may be revised from time to time as required. Parents must pay the applicable amount.
- No concession is provided just because a third child is being educated in the same school.

* Withdrawals and Leave of Absence
- A month's notice of withdrawal must be given in writing and acknowledged by the office. Otherwise, one month's fees in lieu of notice, in addition to Transfer Certificate fees and fees for the period the student's name remains on the rolls, will be charged.
- No student may leave the school compound during class hours without written permission from the Principal.
- Full-day or part-day leave is granted only for a serious reason and on a prior written application from the parent or guardian.
- For sickness or other unforeseen circumstances, a student returning to school must present a leave note and show the teacher-in-charge the Regularity Record duly signed by the parent.
- All students should attend school on the first day of each session. Students who extend their vacation or remain absent without the Principal's prior permission for more than 10 days may have their names struck off the rolls.
- Medical leave for more than 3 days must be accompanied by a medical certificate from an MBBS-qualified doctor showing the doctor's registration number. Leave applications must be signed by the parent or, in their absence, the guardian directly responsible for the student. Failure to bring such an application may result in the student being sent home.
- A student absent for 10 or more days for an unexplained reason may have their name struck off the rolls without notice. If readmitted, it will be treated as a fresh admission.
- Assessments and terminal examinations are held to register student progress, and the marks obtained are considered when deciding promotion.
- Students in Classes I–XII are governed by CBSE, Delhi promotion rules.

* Recommendations to Parents/Guardians
- Parents are expected to cooperate with school authorities in enforcing punctuality, regularity, and discipline; ensuring children prepare their lessons; and taking an active interest in school activities.
- Teachers may write occasional reports in the school diary. Parents should check the diary and sign reports regularly.
- Parents should sign students' notebooks at least once every fortnight and sign periodic progress reports or similar documents when requested. They should check homework, leave, and absence records.
- If a student is likely to be absent due to illness for a longer period, the Principal must be informed within a week.
- When writing to the Principal, parents/guardians should include the student's name, class, and section.
- Parents/guardians should notify the school of any change in their addresses or telephone numbers.
- Parents/guardians and other persons may not meet students or interview teachers during school hours without the Principal's permission. For urgent matters, the Vice-Principal/Headmistress or staff may be seen during free periods with prior permission and an appointment arranged beforehand.

- Parents should avoid criticizing the school or teachers, especially in front of children. Complaints or observations should be made confidentially to the Principal for clarification or remedial action.
- Parent-Teacher Meeting attendance is required. Continuous absence may require parents to explain in writing to the Principal.

***PROJECT SUMMARY FOR THE SCHOOL CHATBOT***

The St. Anthony's Sr. Sec. School AI chatbot website was developed by Manas Gupta, a Class XII PCM student of the school. It is a full-stack AI application designed to make school information easier to access through a conversational interface. Its development journey included an early Python/Streamlit prototype and a later separated frontend/backend architecture using web technologies, Python Flask, Google's Gemini API, Vercel, Render, Git, and GitHub. Manas worked through integration, configuration, API, and deployment challenges while developing and presenting the project.
